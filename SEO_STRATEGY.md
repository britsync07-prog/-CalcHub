# Everyday Calculator Hub — Comprehensive Technical SEO & Organic Growth Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary & Business Objective
- **Business Model:** Ad-monetized free utility website serving high-intent search queries primarily from Tier-1 English markets (US, CA, UK, AU, NZ).
- **Core Strategy:** Attract sustainable organic traffic by delivering immediate, accurate calculations with zero barriers (no accounts, no paywalls, instant reactive results) paired with structured educational content, Schema.org microdata, and clean internal linking clusters.
- **Conversion / Monetization:** High-viewability, zero-CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) display advertising units with strict dimensions and regulatory compliance tags.

## 3. SEO Information Architecture
- **Max Click Depth:** 2 clicks from homepage to any calculator.
- **Topical Clusters:** Each category page (`/category/:slug`) serves as a thematic hub with semantic anchor links connecting every related tool.
- **Crawlability:** Clean URLs with no query parameter dependencies for core indexable content.
- **Dynamic Meta & JSON-LD:**
  - `WebApplication` schema for every calculator.
  - `FAQPage` schema on all pages with frequently asked questions.
  - `BreadcrumbList` schema showing the hierarchical path.
